What are the differences between Saguaro East and West?

#### Differences in Scenery and Accessibility

According to forum posts, Saguaro West is characterized by a denser saguaro forest, dramatic volcanic hillsides, and two-way roads allowing easy back-and-forth travel. In contrast, Saguaro East offers a paved 8-mile loop drive with trailheads, featuring gentle rolling hills and less dense saguaro growth, which some travelers find less photogenic.
