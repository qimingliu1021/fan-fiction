# Anchorage Events

Experience more every week with sparkling winter nights and long summer days. Cheer on the home team, celebrate with locals at festivals full of Alaska flair or take part in time-honored traditions. Discover special programs that bring you closer to the state’s culture. Enjoy world-class performers, hometown theater and rising musicians on stages across Anchorage.

Submit your event **[here](https://www.anchorage.net/events/submit-an-event/)**.  
Stay informed about local happenings. Sign up for the Anchorage Local E-newsletter **[here](https://www.anchorage.net/for-alaskans/anchorage-for-locals/).**  
Download a print copy of the monthly calendar **[here](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/anchorage/November_Calendar_419bb2b9-2db9-4124-b274-bab53785caf1.pdf).**
