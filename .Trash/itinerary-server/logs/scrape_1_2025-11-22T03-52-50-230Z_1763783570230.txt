## This November’s best events

[

### Astra Lumina

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/astra-lumina)

*   Things to do
*   Rancho Palos Verdes/Rolling Hills Estates
*   Until Dec 31, 2025
    
*   Recommended
    

[

![Astra Lumina](https://media.timeout.com/images/105957605/750/422/image.jpg "Astra Lumina")

![Astra Lumina](https://media.timeout.com/images/105957605/750/562/image.jpg "Astra Lumina")

Photograph: Time Out/Michael Juliano

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/astra-lumina)

Stroll through a garden illuminated by celestial-inspired lights during this year-end event at South Coast Botanic Garden. There’s admittedly nothing Christmassy nor even wintry about this hour-long Palos Verdes trail, yet its nine stellar installations are the most cosmically mesmerizing of the budding after-dark [botanical garden](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/the-best-botanical-gardens-and-hidden-oases-in-los-angeles) shows that’ve come to blanket L.A. toward the end of the year.

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/astra-lumina)

[Buy ticket](https://fave.co/49M1IQ2)

[

### Spider Pavilion

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/spider-pavilion)

*   Things to do
*   Exhibitions
*   USC/Exposition Park
*   Until Nov 30, 2025
    

[

![Spider Pavilion](https://media.timeout.com/images/101767697/750/422/image.jpg "Spider Pavilion")

![Spider Pavilion](https://media.timeout.com/images/101767697/750/562/image.jpg "Spider Pavilion")

Photograph: Jakob N. Layman

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/spider-pavilion)

Face your fears and head to the Natural History Museum’s Spider Pavilion, where you can observe several hundred orbweaver spiders in a living exhibit just outside of the museum. Scared the spiders might be hard to spot in the wild? Fret not. In previous iterations, we’ve spotted ones about the size of an adult’s palm. Gulp. (But don’t worry: The scariest ones are in enclosed habitats.) 

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/spider-pavilion)

[Buy ticket](https://nhm.org/plan-your-visit/nhm-buy-tickets)

Advertising

[

### Ice at Santa Monica

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/ice-at-santa-monica-1)

*   Things to do
*   Ice skating
*   Santa Monica
*   Until Jan 19, 2026
    

[

![Ice at Santa Monica](https://media.timeout.com/images/105942909/750/422/image.jpg "Ice at Santa Monica")

![Ice at Santa Monica](https://media.timeout.com/images/105942909/750/562/image.jpg "Ice at Santa Monica")

Photograph: Courtesy Downtown Santa Monica, Inc.

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/ice-at-santa-monica-1)

Located just blocks from the ocean, Ice in downtown Santa Monica brings a bit of winter to the comfortable coastal city. The 8,000-square-foot outdoor rink runs daily from November to mid-January on the corner of Fifth Street and Arizona Avenue (less than a 10-minute walk from the E Line). Tickets for an hourlong slot ($24) include skate rentals, and you can book private parties and fire pits if you’re looking for something a bit more premium. Look out for cozy treats for sale, plus special events like classical Sunday afternoons and a sparkly shindig for Taylor Swift’s birthday (Dec 13).

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/ice-at-santa-monica-1)

[Buy ticket](https://iceatsantamonica.com/)

[

### Shepard Fairey: Out of Print

](/los-angeles/art/shepard-fairey-out-of-print)

*   Art
*   Galleries
*   La Brea
*   Until Jan 11, 2026
    
*   Recommended
    

[

![Shepard Fairey: Out of Print](https://media.timeout.com/images/106342127/750/422/image.jpg "Shepard Fairey: Out of Print")

![Shepard Fairey: Out of Print](https://media.timeout.com/images/106342127/750/562/image.jpg "Shepard Fairey: Out of Print")

Photograph: Courtesy Beyond the Streets

](/los-angeles/art/shepard-fairey-out-of-print)

You probably know of Shepard Fairey through “Obey Giant” stickers and later his Obama “Hope” poster, but his body of work comprises so much more than that. A new exhibition at Beyond the Streets explores the artist’s relationship with printmaking, displaying more than 400 of his original screen prints—including some rare and historic editions and hybrid works that combine screen printing with stenciling—and offering a behind‑the‑scenes look at Fairey’s methods.

[Read more](/los-angeles/art/shepard-fairey-out-of-print)

Advertising

[

### The Huntington Holiday Market

](/los-angeles/shopping/the-huntington-holiday-market)

*   Shopping
*   Markets and fairs
*   San Marino
*   Nov 22, 2025
    

[

![The Huntington Holiday Market](https://media.timeout.com/images/105705406/750/422/image.jpg "The Huntington Holiday Market")

![The Huntington Holiday Market](https://media.timeout.com/images/105705406/750/562/image.jpg "The Huntington Holiday Market")

Photograph: Michael Juliano

](/los-angeles/shopping/the-huntington-holiday-market)

We could spend hours wandering the Huntington’s gardens—and about just as long in its wonderful gift shop. Well, here’s an excuse to spend even _more_ time there: this one-day-only artisan pop-up market, which brings more than three dozen art, home, jewelry, craft and fashion vendors to the Huntington Store, Rose Hills Garden Court, Rothenberg Patio and Haaga Hall. We recommend viennoiserie pastries from Salted Butter Company, old-Hollywood-inspired makeup from Bésame Cosmetics, eco-friendly stuffed toys from Dear Mello and jewelry adorned with hand-pressed wildflowers from Cast & Clover. Admission is free and doesn’t require a ticket for the gardens.

[Read more](/los-angeles/shopping/the-huntington-holiday-market)

[

### Sabrina Carpenter

](/los-angeles/music/sabrina-carpenter)

*   Music
*   Pop
*   South Park
*   Until Nov 23, 2025
    

[

![Sabrina Carpenter](https://media.timeout.com/images/106148314/750/422/image.jpg "Sabrina Carpenter")

![Sabrina Carpenter](https://media.timeout.com/images/106148314/750/562/image.jpg "Sabrina Carpenter")

Photograph: lev radin / Shutterstock.com

](/los-angeles/music/sabrina-carpenter)

Did last year’s [Coachella](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/music/coachella) catapult Sabrina Carpenter into the spotlight? Or was the onetime Disney Channel personality already on the precipice of pop superstardom? Ponder that as you wait in the ticketing queue for the “Espresso” singer and now-Grammy winner’s six shows at the Crypto.com Arena, a reprise of last year’s “Short N’ Sweet Tour.” Ravyn Lenae and Amber Mark join as supporting acts. Tickets go on sale Friday, March 7, at 10am.

[Read more](/los-angeles/music/sabrina-carpenter)

[Buy ticket](https://entertainment.timeout.com/results-event?event=Sabrina+Carpenter&pid=68914)

Advertising

### L.A. on Wheels Day

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/l-a-on-wheels-day)

*   Things to do
*   Festivals
*   USC/Exposition Park
*   Nov 22, 2025
    

[

![L.A. on Wheels Day](https://media.timeout.com/images/106086568/750/422/image.jpg "L.A. on Wheels Day")

![L.A. on Wheels Day](https://media.timeout.com/images/106086568/750/562/image.jpg "L.A. on Wheels Day")

Photograph: Michael Juliano for Time Out

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/l-a-on-wheels-day)

The Natural History Museum promises a _wheely_ good time at this festival that celebrates what makes Los Angeles run. The in-person event—part of the museum’s online [Being L.A.](https://nhm.org/la-on-wheels) initiative—will offer activities including car and bike shows, skate demos, hands-on art activities, discussions and storytelling, which are included with museum admission. At 3pm, a slide-show presentation by retro pop-culture comedian Charles Phoenix exploring Southern California in the ’50s and ’60s requires a separate $30 [ticket](https://nhm.org/calendar/charles-phoenix-southern-california-50s-60s).

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/l-a-on-wheels-day)

[Buy ticket](https://tickets.nhmlac.org/orders/527/tickets)

[

### Bob Baker Marionette Theater’s Holiday Special: Winter Wonderland

](/los-angeles/theater/bob-baker-marionette-theaters-holiday-on-strings)

*   Puppet shows
*   Highland Park
*   Until Jan 18, 2026
    
*   Recommended
    

[

![Bob Baker Marionette Theater’s Holiday Special: Winter Wonderland](https://media.timeout.com/images/105724634/750/422/image.jpg "Bob Baker Marionette Theater’s Holiday Special: Winter Wonderland")

![Bob Baker Marionette Theater’s Holiday Special: Winter Wonderland](https://media.timeout.com/images/105724634/750/562/image.jpg "Bob Baker Marionette Theater’s Holiday Special: Winter Wonderland")

Photograph: Courtesy Lisa Whiteman

](/los-angeles/theater/bob-baker-marionette-theaters-holiday-on-strings)

The beloved Bob Baker Marionette Theater’s year-end production, formerly known as _Holiday on Strings_, has been reimagined as a holiday special complete with puppets in party clothes singing carols. Head to the troupe’s Highland Park location for the hour-long show, which follows host Demitrius Nova Twinklestar III on a tour of BBMT’s holiday traditions: You’ll see sledding slopes, caroling penguins, Santa’s workshop and more. For a sensory-friendly version, book tickets to the 4:30pm show on Saturday, December 20.

[Read more](/los-angeles/theater/bob-baker-marionette-theaters-holiday-on-strings)

[Buy ticket](https://ci.ovationtix.com/35787/production/1255396)

Advertising

### Netflix x CicLAvia—Melrose Ave: ‘Stranger Things 5’ One Last Ride

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/ciclavia)

*   Things to do
*   Nov 23, 2025
    
*   Recommended
    

[

![Netflix x CicLAvia—Melrose Ave: ‘Stranger Things 5’ One Last Ride](https://media.timeout.com/images/106342089/750/422/image.jpg "Netflix x CicLAvia—Melrose Ave: ‘Stranger Things 5’ One Last Ride")

![Netflix x CicLAvia—Melrose Ave: ‘Stranger Things 5’ One Last Ride](https://media.timeout.com/images/106342089/750/562/image.jpg "Netflix x CicLAvia—Melrose Ave: ‘Stranger Things 5’ One Last Ride")

Photograph: Courtesy CicLAvia Los Angeles

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/ciclavia)

Locally beloved open-streets event CicLAvia is taking a turn for the stranger this month, celebrating the fifth and final season of the Netflix hit _Stranger Things_. Four miles of Melrose, from Fairfax to Vermont Avenues, will be closed to cars and transformed into the show’s fictional setting of Hawkins, Indiana, in the 1980s. Immersive photo ops, pedal-powered activations, live entertainment, merch and giveaways will further plunge you into the Upside Down before the season airs. You can even pick up a map and embark on your own quest along the way. (And it goes without saying that you should bike or take the [Metro](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/los-angeles-metro-guide) to your desired spot along the route.)

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/ciclavia)

[

### Enchant LA

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/enchant-la)

*   Things to do
*   Ice skating
*   Arcadia
*   Until Dec 28, 2025
    

[

![Enchant LA](https://media.timeout.com/images/106342748/750/422/image.jpg "Enchant LA")

![Enchant LA](https://media.timeout.com/images/106342748/750/562/image.jpg "Enchant LA")

Photograph: Navid Baraty, courtesy Enchant LA

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/enchant-la)

Not to be confused with [Enchanted Forest of Light](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/enchanted-forest-of-light) at Descanso Gardens, this newcomer to L.A.’s [Christmas-lights](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/christmas-lights-in-los-angeles-where-to-find-holiday-lights) scene is also located in the San Gabriel Valley—Santa Anita Park, to be precise—and claims to be the largest Christmas-lights maze in the world. In addition to its lit-up labyrinth with a 100-foot tree of lights as its centerpiece, the one-stop holiday extravaganza also boasts ice skating, an ice slide, a Ferris wheel, mini golf, a shopping village, visits with Santa and festive treats and drinks. 

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/enchant-la)

[Buy ticket](https://tickets.enchantchristmas.com/los-angeles-ca-santa-anita-park/calendar)

Advertising

### A Nostalgic Night With Macaulay Culkin

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/a-nostalgic-night-with-macaulay-culkin)

*   Things to do
*   Film events
*   Long Beach
*   Nov 22, 2025
    

[

![A Nostalgic Night With Macaulay Culkin](https://media.timeout.com/images/105847246/750/422/image.jpg "A Nostalgic Night With Macaulay Culkin")

![A Nostalgic Night With Macaulay Culkin](https://media.timeout.com/images/105847246/750/562/image.jpg "A Nostalgic Night With Macaulay Culkin")

Photograph: Courtesy Disney

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/a-nostalgic-night-with-macaulay-culkin)

Planning your holiday-season entertainment? Don’t forget Kevin! While Walt Disney Concert Hall’s [special screenings](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/home-alone-in-concert) of holiday classic _Home Alone_—complete with live score—are an annual tradition, a couple of weeks beforehand, you can hang out with Macaulay Culkin himself at Long Beach’s Terrace Theater, following a 35th anniversary screening of the nostalgic 1990 film. The actor will stick around for an interview and audience Q&A, where he’ll share behind-the-scenes stories from the set and his favorite memories from filming. 

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/a-nostalgic-night-with-macaulay-culkin)

[Buy ticket](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/0B006315D21E3579)

### New Original Works (NOW) Festival

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/new-original-works-festival)

*   Things to do
*   Festivals
*   Downtown
*   Until Nov 22, 2025
    
*   Recommended
    

[

![New Original Works (NOW) Festival](https://media.timeout.com/images/105807277/750/422/image.jpg "New Original Works (NOW) Festival")

![New Original Works (NOW) Festival](https://media.timeout.com/images/105807277/750/562/image.jpg "New Original Works (NOW) Festival")

Photograph: carlos.araujo / Shutterstock.com

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/new-original-works-festival)

See what’s cutting-edge in the city’s vibrant performing arts scene at REDCAT’s 22nd annual festival, where it turns its theater into a creative artistic laboratory for Los Angeles artists à la its namesake institution, CalArts. Each of the three weekends boasts unique, daring and distinctive performances of new original works for the stage—from dance, theater and music to multimedia performance art. If you can’t make it to the fest in person, the performances will also be livestreamed.

[Read more](/los-angeles/things-to-do/new-original-works-festival)

[Buy ticket](https://www.redcat.org/events)

### Robert Therrien: This Is a Story

](/los-angeles/art/robert-therrien-this-is-a-story)

*   Art
*   Sculpture
*   Downtown
*   Nov 22, 2025Apr 5, 2026
    

[

![Robert Therrien: This Is a Story](https://media.timeout.com/images/106280813/750/422/image.jpg "Robert Therrien: This Is a Story")

![Robert Therrien: This Is a Story](https://media.timeout.com/images/106280813/750/562/image.jpg "Robert Therrien: This Is a Story")

Photograph: Courtesy of the Glenstone Museum, image by Joshua White/JWPictures.com

](/los-angeles/art/robert-therrien-this-is-a-story)

Robert Therrien’s _Under the Table_ has long been one of the most popular pieces in the Broad’s collection (you know the one—the giant table and chairs that you ask your friend to snap a photo of as you stand underneath). Well now the museum is hosting the largest-ever solo exhibition of the artist’s work, displaying more than 120 pieces, including many that have never been shown in museums before. Expect more huge housewares and striking works, plus some intimate drawings and surprises from the late L.A.-based artist. The specially ticketed show will fill the first-floor galleries through April 5, 2026.

[Read more](/los-angeles/art/robert-therrien-this-is-a-story)

[Buy ticket](https://ticketing.thebroad.org/events/50d9eaf5-cbba-b8d0-32d4-84695c590689)

### Venice Winter Fest

](/los-angeles/things-to-do/the-venice-fest)

*   Things to do
*   Festivals

The Getty Villa reopens to the public five and a half months after its Palisades Fire closure with this international loan exhibition dedicated to the Greek Mycenaean civilization and the kingdom of Pylos, which Homer immortalized in the _Iliad_ and _Odyssey_. It’s the first major museum show in North America devoted to the Late Bronze Age Mycenaeans. See treasures excavators unearthed from Messenia, the Palace of Nestor and burial sites including the tomb of the Griffin Warrior (1450 BCE)—think clay tablets, gold cups, ornate weapons and tiny signets and sealstones adorned with awe-inspiring amounts of detail.

### GAME ON! Science, Sports & Play

](/los-angeles/museums/game-on-science-sports-play)

*   Museums
*   Science and technology
*   USC/Exposition Park
*   Until May 12, 2026
    

[

![GAME ON! Science, Sports & Play](https://media.timeout.com/images/106269112/750/422/image.jpg "GAME ON! Science, Sports & Play")

![GAME ON! Science, Sports & Play](https://media.timeout.com/images/106269112/750/562/image.jpg "GAME ON! Science, Sports & Play")

Photograph: Courtesy California Science Center

](/los-angeles/museums/game-on-science-sports-play)

The California Science Center is inviting kids to get in the game with a new 17,000-square-foot exhibition about the power of play and the human body in motion. Besides teaching about the science behind sports, it also offers interactive challenges and video coaching from a team of Los Angeles-based mentor athletes including dancer Debbie Allen, the Dodgers’ World Series hero Freddie Freeman, Olympic medalist softball player Rachel Garcia and more. And for the first time ever, the center has commissioned public art—all by local artists—to complement the exhibition, including a Dodgers mural by Gustavo Zermeño Jr. The free exhibition will run at the Science Center through the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

### Director’s Inspiration: Bong Joon Ho

](/los-angeles/museums/directors-inspiration-bong-joon-ho)

*   Museums
*   Movies and TV
*   Miracle Mile
*   Until Jan 10, 2027
    

[

![Director’s Inspiration: Bong Joon Ho](https://media.timeout.com/images/106251546/750/422/image.jpg "Director’s Inspiration: Bong Joon Ho")

![Director’s Inspiration: Bong Joon Ho](https://media.timeout.com/images/106251546/750/562/image.jpg "Director’s Inspiration: Bong Joon Ho")

Photograph: Lee Jaehyuk, courtesy Netflix

](/los-angeles/museums/directors-inspiration-bong-joon-ho)

Right on the heels of the release of his new film, _Mickey 17_, director Bong Joon Ho steps into the spotlight at the Academy Museum’s latest “Director’s Spotlight” exhibition (past subjects have included Spike Lee and Agnès Varda). The first-ever museum show dedicated to the Oscar-winning South Korean filmmaker will trace Ho’s career, creative process and cinematic influences. See over 100 storyboards, research materials, posters, concept art, creature models, props and on-set photos from the director’s archive and personal collection. On opening day, March 23, catch screenings of _Okja_ (2pm) and _Parasite_ (7:30pm) in the David Geffen Theater—Ho himself will be there in person.
