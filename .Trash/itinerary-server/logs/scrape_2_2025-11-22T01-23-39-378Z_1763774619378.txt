## Oklahoma City Attractions

The Midwest is more than flat terrain and cold winter temperatures, and Oklahoma City is here to prove just that. This Oklahoma destination is proud to offer sports, art, awesome cuisine, and lots of family-friendly activities. If you’re interested in an adventure in the Heartland, OKC is an excellent venue for it. Attractions in Oklahoma City range from the historic to the chic, and everything in between. Whether you’re on a family road trip or flying in for a long weekend, Oklahoma City aims to impress.

#### Areas & Neighborhoods in Oklahoma City

Oklahoma City is a melting pot of art, culture, and lots of good food. Each neighborhood in this Midwest city brings something different to the table, and every district welcomes visitors and locals alike to explore and fall in love with what is has to offer.

**Downtown** — Downtown Oklahoma City lives up to the expectations you would set for the center of the city. Its districts include Bricktown on the lower east side, where you can enjoy tons of live music and attractions like the Chickasaw Bricktown Ballpark. If you’re look for the most things to do in Oklahoma City per capita, downtown may be the ticket.

**Uptown** — Uptown Oklahoma City is also considered the historic district. In addition to the farmers market, this part of town also proudly hosts a variety of small businesses, such as independent boutiques. It’s also here that you will find the Historic Tower Theater.

**Midtown** — Midtown is just north of downtown, and it definitely competes in the food and entertainment departments. The area is growing constantly, but you will still find lots of vintage shops and other eclectic businesses around.

#### Things to See in Oklahoma City

Before you hit the town with your sneakers or dancing shoes, stop and smell the roses. The Myriad Botanical Gardens in OKC offers 15 acres of manicured public space, and you’re welcome to bring your camera and even your off-leash dog. Things to see in Oklahoma City also include the architecture of places such as St. Joseph Old Cathedral and the Overholser Mansion. When you’re ready to be entertained, some of the top things to do in Oklahoma City, OK happen on stage. Sit back, relax, and watch a rousing one-man show or a fun musical at venues like the Civic Center Music Hall and Riverwind Casino Showplace Theatre.

#### Things to Do & Attractions in Oklahoma City

If you’re into the history of the city, fun things to do in Oklahoma City won’t leave you hanging. Visit the State Capitol and governor’s mansion for a quick peek into state government. You can also spend a day walking the Oklahoma City Zoo and learning more about elephants, gorillas, and other creatures from around the globe. Meander through the Paseo Arts District to catch a local festival or take an art class while you’re in town. After you explore your creative side, head to the Chesapeake Energy Arena to cheer on the Oklahoma City Thunder® NBA team.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Oklahoma City worth visiting?

Whether you're here to experience the local charm and cultural attractions or try unique foods and venture into nature, there's no shortage of things to do in Oklahoma City.

How do I spend a day in Oklahoma City?

Oklahoma City Zoo and Frontier City are two good places to begin your sightseeing adventures. Remember to explore the dining and retail options in Oklahoma City while you're in town too.

What is the top tourist attraction in Oklahoma City?

The top tourist attraction in Oklahoma City is Oklahoma City Zoo, so be sure to add it to your sightseeing list.

Is three days enough to stay in Oklahoma City?

Three days will give you enough time to check out many city attractions, like Oklahoma City Zoo, Hurricane Harbour Oaklahoma City and Oklahoma City Museum of Art. The average stay in Oklahoma City is two days. If you feel the urge to extend your escape, Norman isn't far away.

Is Oklahoma City a walkable city?

It depends on what you want to do and see in Oklahoma City. Several key sights, such as Oklahoma City Museum of Art and First Americans Museum, are reachable on foot. If you're up for a wander through some of Oklahoma City's best neighbourhoods, Downtown Oklahoma City is 0.64 kilometres away from downtown, while Bricktown is 0.32 kilometres away from the city centre and also within walking distance.

What are some activities to do in Oklahoma City with kids?

Deciding what to do in Oklahoma City with kids has never been easier. Whether you're travelling with teenagers or little ones, there are lots of family-friendly attractions to keep them entertained here.

  

*   [Bricktown Oklahoma City Scavenger Hunt](/things-to-do/bricktown-oklahoma-city-scavenger-hunt.a50661899.activity-details)

What to do in Oklahoma City for adults?

Whether you're a culture enthusiast, food lover or thrill seeker, there are loads of things to do in Oklahoma City for adults including Paycom Centre and Oklahoma State Fair Arena.

Are there free things to do in Oklahoma City?

One of the top ways to discover free things to do in Oklahoma City is to chat to a local tour guide. You'll get insider views on cool neighbourhoods, parks and other highlights you can enjoy without opening your wallet. Celebration Station is one place that doesn't have an entrance fee.
