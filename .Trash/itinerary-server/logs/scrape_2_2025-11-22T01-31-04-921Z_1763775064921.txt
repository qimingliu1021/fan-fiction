# Things to Do in Tucson This Weekend

Looking for [things to do](https://www.visittucson.org/things-to-do/) and events in Tucson this weekend? Whether you’re into [live music](https://www.visittucson.org/events/concerts-and-live-music/), [outdoor recreation](https://www.visittucson.org/things-to-do/outdoors/), [family-friendly entertainment](https://www.visittucson.org/things-to-do/arts-and-entertainment/), [performing arts](https://www.visittucson.org/things-to-do/arts-and-entertainment/performing-arts/) and [cultural events](https://www.visittucson.org/things-to-do/history-and-culture/), or sporting events, there're plenty of things to do this weekend. If you're planning for a future trip, [check out our full calendar of events](https://www.visittucson.org/events/).
