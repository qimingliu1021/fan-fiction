# 26 Memorable Things To Do And Events In Los Angeles This November 2025

Say goodbye to [pumpkins](https://secretlosangeles.com/best-pumpkin-patches-los-angeles/) and hello to [holiday lights.](https://secretlosangeles.com/holiday-events-la/) It’s the month when Halloween simmers down and we begin to gear up for the winter holidays, and **L.A. is still brimming with exciting events to keep you busy this November.**

Whether it’s watching the greenery turn different shades of [orange and yellow](https://secretlosangeles.com/best-places-to-see-fall-foliage-around-la/), checking out the L.A. Auto Show, or catching some early [holiday lights](https://secretlosangeles.com/best-places-holiday-lights-around-la/), **November 2025 is set to be a month to remember in Los Angeles.**

## 1\. [Kick off November by celebrating Día de los Muertos](https://secretlosangeles.com/dia-de-los-muertos/)

![City of Long Beach Dia de los muertos](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/13022637/dia-de-los-muertos.jpeg)

City of Long Beach

Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is traditionally celebrated on **November 1st and 2nd.** We’re looking forward to some **beautiful events in the first week of the month** including the cultural installation at Avenue 50 Studio, the festival at historic Olvera Street, and the parade in Downtown Long Beach. [See the full list of local Día de los Muertos events here.](https://secretlosangeles.com/dia-de-los-muertos/)

## 2\. [Experience an unforgettable night of Música Mexicana, Rap, and Corridos with Fuerza Regida](https://secretlosangeles.com/dont-fall-in-love-fest-2025/)

![Fuerza Regida](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/08100930/Dont-Fall-In-Love-Fest.jpg)

Courtesy of Don’t Fall In Love Fest.

Fuerza Regida is uniting the biggest names in música Mexicana, rap, and corridos for their **_Don’t Fall In Love Fest_**, returning to their hometown in San Bernardino. The festival stands as a testament to unity, creativity, and pride, bringing **música mexicana and rap together on one stage** to celebrate the voices that shape today’s Latin and urban music scenes. [Get the details here!](https://secretlosangeles.com/dont-fall-in-love-fest-2025/)

**🗓️When**: Saturday, Nov. 22 at 12:00 PM  
**📍Where:** NOS Events Center

## 3\. [Walk among the cosmos at Astra Lumina](https://feverup.com/m/468143)

![A couple walks past light displays](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/13022301/t1.jpg)

Image by Astra Lumina

**Astra Lumina**, which debuted in 2022, is back at South Coast Botanic Garden with a **stunning array of celestial-inspired displays.** This multimedia experience from the renowned studio Moment Factory combines projections, lighting, music, and a captivating story into an unforgettable **night walk among the cosmos.** 

📍 **Location****:** South Coast Botanic Garden, 26300 Crenshaw Blvd, Palos Verdes Estates, CA 90274  
**🎫 [Get your tickets here!](https://feverup.com/m/468143)**

## 4\. [Enjoy An Enchanting Candlelight Concert](https://feverup.com/los-angeles/candlelight)

![The Immanuel Presbyterian Church in candlelight](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/13030051/Temp-1200x800-9-1024x683.png)

Candlelight Concerts

The wildly popular **Candlelight Concert** series has never ceased to captivate audiences across the globe. The series brings you talented musicians who perform live concerts in beautiful settings — all bathed in Candlelight. November shows feature some of the series’ favorite tribute performances, including fan-favorite hits by **Pink Floyd, Queen, Adele, The Beatles, Joe Hisaishi, and Taylor Swift.**

## 5\. **Did someone say Vegas trip? Launch into a galactic “must see” adventure at Interstellar Arc in Las Vegas**

![The interior of the Interstellar Arc building](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/18150123/image3-1.jpg)

Interstellar Arc

If you’re looking for a weekend getaway to Vegas, **Interstellar Arc** is a new mixed-reality experience that blasts you into space as you search for humanity’s next home. It’s something you have to experience to truly understand, and luckily tickets are **currently 30% off.** [Click here to learn more about the experience and the limited-time Cyber Monday deal.](https://tickets.interstellararc.com/events/59759ab8-862e-3bcb-a753-7c4277310e82?tg=d0a53c01-75a8-9371-7b25-6bad0a613061)

![📆](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/svg/1f4c6.svg) **When:** Open daily from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.  
![📍](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/svg/1f4cd.svg) **Where:** AREA15 – Zone 2: The Terminals (3072 Rigel Ave, Building 170, Las Vegas)

## 8\. [Catch _Tarantino Live_ at the stunning CineVita venue](https://tarantinolive.com/los-angeles/)

![A woman holds katanas onstage](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/31120351/1-8.jpg)

Tarantino Live

_Tarantino Live_ reimagines iconic moments from films like **Kill Bill, Pulp Fiction,** and **Django Unchained** with a live rock soundtrack, cinematic storytelling, and bold performances that unfold around and throughout the audience. It all takes place at **CineVita**, a live entertainment venue with a central stage that is surrounded by the audience, resulting in performances that are intimate, immersive, and up-close. [Click here for tickets & more info!](https://tarantinolive.com/los-angeles/)

Whether you’re a sports fan or not, the buzz of a full crowd in the **City of Champions cannot be missed.** It’s fall after all, which means catching a Kings game is a must! Go Kings, Go — [check out the Kings schedule here](https://www.nhl.com/kings/).

## 11\. Prepare to be mesmerized by gorgeous rides at the LA Auto Show

## 13\. [Head to this twinkling night market at a century-old lavender farm near L.A.](https://secretlosangeles.com/123-lavender-farm-christmas-market/)

![A grove of oak trees decorated in gold lights at night.](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/13022946/Screen-Shot-2023-10-01-at-4.20.22-PM.png)

Image courtesy of Highland Springs Ranch & Inn

The **picturesque 123 Farm**, known for its beautiful swaths of violet [lavender flowers](https://secretlosangeles.com/lavender-farms-los-angeles/) over the summer, has a **stunning night market**. Its towering trees and fields have been 1,000 years in the making and you can continue to enjoy its beauty post the summer blooms.

## 14\. [Trot and gobble at this Thanksgiving 5K for a good cause](https://secretlosangeles.com/turkey-trot-race-dtla/)

![Turkey Trot Los Angeles](https://offloadmedia.feverup.com/secretlosangeles.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/13022649/397094721_825478396250032_7954839995817779346_n.jpeg)

Image courtesy of Turkey Trot Los Angeles

Step into the [Thanksgiving](https://secretlosangeles.com/thanksgiving-day-los-angeles/) morning with a heart full of gratitude and a stride of determination. Join the community for the annual **Turkey Trot Los Angeles** and be a part of the unforgettable stride where thousands of runners and walkers unite. Lace up those sneakers and gobble up some miles together for a good cause — and fundraise for The Midnight Mission.

**🦃 Details:** Participate in the 5k, 10k, or a .5 mile Widdle Wobble for kids. Everyone gets a race shirt, sparkly finisher medal, and bib.  
**🏃‍♂️ Date:** Thanksgiving morning – Nov. 27, 2025, from 8-11am  
****📍Location:**** [Downtown Los Angeles](https://secretlosangeles.com/things-to-do-downtown-la/). The race starts and finishes in front of City Hall at 200 N Spring St, Los Angeles, CA 90012.
