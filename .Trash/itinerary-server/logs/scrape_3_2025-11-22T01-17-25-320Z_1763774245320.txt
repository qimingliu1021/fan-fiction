# Newark, NJ Events Calendar

#### Welcome to our vibrant Events page, where you can discover the pulse of our community!

Whether you're a local looking for the latest happenings or a visitor seeking unique experiences, our city has something for everyone. From [cultural celebrations](https://www.newarkhappening.com/events/performing-arts/) to [family-friendly gatherings](https://www.newarkhappening.com/things-to-do/family-fun/), our calendar is packed with exciting [events](https://www.newarkhappening.com/events/) year-round.

Explore the rich tapestry of our [Annual Events](https://www.newarkhappening.com/events/annual-events/), where tradition meets innovation, and discover the heart of our city's culture. Dive into the [Seasonal Festivals](https://www.newarkhappening.com/events/festivals/) that bring a touch of magic to every time of year, as our city comes alive with colors, music, and festivities.

Join us as we celebrate the spirit of Newark, where every event tells a story and creates memories to last a lifetime. Explore further to find details about upcoming events, past highlights, and more. Let's make memories together!
