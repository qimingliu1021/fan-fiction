## What is the ZENNI Expo & Fiesta?

For three days, over 9,000 El Tour de Tucson participants, as well as the general public and spectators, interact with exhibitors, from big brands to local shops. Merchandise sales, product demos, giveaways, panels, contests — it’s all at the ZENNI Expo & Fiesta.

![](https://d2mkojm4rk40ta.cloudfront.net/us-east-1-src/prod/clientUploads/2023-04/15/1/143850/60cf142e-fdd7-4b29-9e9e-93071a3f1cbb-bKoYsq.jpg)

**El Tour's ZENNI Expo & Fiesta Hours & Location  
**Tucson Convention Center's Exhibition Hall in Downtown Tucson  
260 South Church Avenue, Tucson

*   THURSDAY – Noon – 8pm
*   FRIDAY – Noon – 8pm
*   SATURDAY – 6am – 5pm

![](https://d2mkojm4rk40ta.cloudfront.net/us-east-1-src/prod/clientUploads/2024-05/25/1/143850/a6f39739-ecf3-4be5-b62c-2ea591a86c19-bMuJvi.png)

**Packet Pickup Hours**

*   Thursday  – November 20th, Noon – 8pm
*   Friday – November 21st,  Noon – 8pm

**Safety & Security Meetings (Orientation Meetings)**

Thursday & Friday,  check the schedule as we get closer to the event for times. 

Highly recommended short sessions giving you information about route conditions, the ride in general and is your opportunity to ask questions!  Every half hour.

**Platinum Meeting**

Thursday and Friday, 1pm and 6pm

If you earned platinum designation, you must attend one of these meetings to get the pass that allows you to line up in the platinum corral.
