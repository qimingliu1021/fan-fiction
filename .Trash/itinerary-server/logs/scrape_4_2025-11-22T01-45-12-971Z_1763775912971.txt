# Mental Health Conferences in Alaska for 2025

Discover mental health conferences in Alaska, where innovative care solutions meet the stunning landscapes of the state. From Anchorage to Fairbanks, these events bring together professionals dedicated to improving mental well-being across Alaska’s communities. These are the ones in the year 2025.

[All Conferences](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [Upcoming Conferences](/conferences/2025/alaska/upcoming/) [Past Conferences](/conferences/2025/alaska/past/) [Calendar](/conferences/2025/alaska/calendar/)

#### Conferences

Hopefully one of these conferences aligns with your interests (and your locale).

#### January

### [2025 Alaska Health Summit](/conferences/2025/alaska/K1E6/2025-alaska-health-summit/)

The 2025 Alaska Health Summit is a premier conference held in Anchorage, AK, dedicated to promoting the health and well-being of Alaskans, with a focus on equitable healthcare access.

January 28–30, 2025 Alaska Public Health Association [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/K1E6/2025-alaska-health-summit/)

#### March

### [2025 Spring Members’ and Affiliates’ Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/5QAO/2025-spring-members-and-affiliates-conference/)

The Alaska Behavioral Health Association is hosting its annual Spring Conference in Juneau, Alaska. This event focuses on legislative advocacy and field innovations, providing an opportunity for professionals to engage with state leaders.

March 4–5, 2025 Alaska Behavioral Health Association [Juneau, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/5QAO/2025-spring-members-and-affiliates-conference/)

### [Stone Soup Group 2025 Parent Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/PXIZ/stone-soup-group-2025-parent-conference/)

The Stone Soup Group 2025 Parent Conference is an annual event in Anchorage, AK, designed to support parents, caregivers, and professionals working with children and youth who experience disabilities. It features keynote speakers, workshops, and networking opportunities focused on empowering families and promoting inclusivity.

March 19–21, 2025 Stone Soup Group [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/PXIZ/stone-soup-group-2025-parent-conference/)

#### April

### [2025 Alaska Psychiatric Association CME Meeting](/conferences/2025/alaska/2VU1/2025-alaska-psychiatric-association-cme-meeting/)

The 2025 Alaska Psychiatric Association CME Meeting is set to take place in Girdwood, AK. This event will be held at the Alyeska Hotel and offers an opportunity for professionals in the mental health field to engage in educational sessions and networking.

April 4–6, 2025 Alaska Psychiatric Association [Girdwood, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/2VU1/2025-alaska-psychiatric-association-cme-meeting/)

### [2025 Alaska Psychiatric Association CME Meeting](/conferences/2025/alaska/7NR6/2025-alaska-psychiatric-association-cme-meeting/)

The 2025 Alaska Psychiatric Association CME Meeting is a professional gathering for mental health experts, taking place in Girdwood, AK. It offers a platform for education and networking among psychiatric professionals.

April 4–6, 2025 Alaska Psychiatric Association [Girdwood, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/7NR6/2025-alaska-psychiatric-association-cme-meeting/)

#### May

### [52nd Annual 2025 National Association for Rural Mental Health Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/F1D9/52nd-annual-2025-national-association-for-rural-mental-health-conference/)

The 2025 National Association for Rural Mental Health Conference will be held in Anchorage, AK, focusing on cultural diversity and strengths in mental well-being.

May 1–3, 2025 National Association for Rural Mental Health [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/F1D9/52nd-annual-2025-national-association-for-rural-mental-health-conference/)

### [52nd Annual 2025 National Association for Rural Mental Health Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/IRSC/52nd-annual-2025-national-association-for-rural-mental-health-conference/)

The 2025 National Association for Rural Mental Health Annual Conference will be held in-person at the Hotel Captain Cook in Anchorage, AK from May 1 to May 3, 2025. The conference will focus on rural policy, clinical practice, and leadership.

May 1–3, 2025 National Association for Rural Mental Health [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/IRSC/52nd-annual-2025-national-association-for-rural-mental-health-conference/)

### [Infant Child and Youth (ICY) Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/5H9O/infant-child-and-youth-icy-conference/)

The Infant Child and Youth (ICY) Conference is held in Anchorage, Alaska, offering professionals opportunities to network and improve care for families.

May 6–8, 2025 UAA Center for Human Development [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/5H9O/infant-child-and-youth-icy-conference/)

### [AK Medicaid and AKBH Spring 2025 Provider Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/KB8E/ak-medicaid-and-akbh-spring-2025-provider-conference/)

The AK Medicaid and AKBH Spring 2025 Provider Conference is a virtual event hosted by Comagine Health in partnership with Alaska Medicaid. It focuses on both physical and behavioral health provider education.

May 7–8, 2025 Comagine Health [Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/KB8E/ak-medicaid-and-akbh-spring-2025-provider-conference/)

#### September

### [2025 Alaska State of Reform Health Policy Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/1VRA/2025-alaska-state-of-reform-health-policy-conference/)

The 2025 Alaska State of Reform Health Policy Conference will be held in Anchorage, Alaska, focusing on healthcare reform and policy discussions.

September 11, 2025 State of Reform [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/1VRA/2025-alaska-state-of-reform-health-policy-conference/)

### [2025 AHHA Annual Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/YOQZ/2025-ahha-annual-conference/)

The 2025 AHHA Annual Conference will be held at Alyeska Resort in Girdwood, Alaska, focusing on current issues impacting Alaska healthcare. It offers networking opportunities and insights from leaders in the field.

September 16–17, 2025 Alaska Hospital & Healthcare Association (AHHA) [Girdwood, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/YOQZ/2025-ahha-annual-conference/)

#### October

### [2025 Fall Members’ and Affiliates’ Conference](/conferences/2025/alaska/W7NY/2025-fall-members-and-affiliates-conference/)

The Alaska Behavioral Health Association is hosting its annual Fall Conference in Anchorage, Alaska, on October 1-2, 2025. The event will feature sessions on field innovations, keynote addresses, and panel discussions with state officials.

October 1–2, 2025 Alaska Behavioral Health Association [Anchorage, Alaska](/conferences/2025/alaska/) [More Details](/conferences/2025/alaska/W7NY/2025-fall-members-and-affiliates-conference/)
