### Event Details

Silent Book Club is a community of book lovers who find joy and relaxation in spending time with friends while reading whatever they want. No more stress to get a book you didn’t even choose finished. Chit chat about what book you are reading (or don’t), grab a drink and/or snack, then cozy on into a chair and get reading. This is a great way to break into that TBR pile you’ve got going!

What does a Silent Book Club meet up look like? Think of it as Introvert Happy Hour!

11am-11:30am-People arrive, order a drink/snack, share what they’re reading, check out the word search contest (maybe win a prize!)

11:30am-12:30pm-Quiet reading hour.

12:30-1pm-Optional socializing, or keep reading. Or dip out and go on your merry way! That’s the beauty of SBC.

SBC Flagstaff will meet every other Saturday in the Bookmans Cafe from 11am-1pm starting February 1, 2025. Please follow our Instagram page @sbcflagstaff to get updates.

### Event Details

We know you love trinkets and trading as much as we do. That’s why were bringing this Trinket Trade event to all Bookmans locations!

Trinkets are fun because they can give personal style to your backpack or be given to someone as a small token to show you care. They connect us to our greater interests and they are a great way to show others that we might enjoy the same things.

**How it works.**

This will be a person to person swap but we’ll start the fun with our own contribution to the pool of trinket treasures. All you have to do is bring in some trinkets that you think others will love. Add a trinket to the table and take a trinket you like, feel free to bring as many as you want but you can only take as many as you leave. Everyone will take turns and we will help facilitate the swap but there will be no exchange of money or transactions with Bookmans for this free event. We believe in the power of trading to connect people over a shared interest and can’t wait to see you for the Trinket Trade!

**What is a trinket?**

A trinket is a small memento or keepsake like buttons, pins, key chains, charms, tiny plushies or little figures.

\*For the most fun possible try to bring trinkets that have a wide appeal and others will truly appreciate.
