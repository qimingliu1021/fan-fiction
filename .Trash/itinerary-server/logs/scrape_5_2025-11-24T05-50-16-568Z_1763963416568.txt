### Movement Lab: Bossa Beats

Movement Lab: Bossa Beats (samba-fusion cardio dance)  
  
A 60-minute class that resets your energy through synchronized movement and rhythm.  
  
Samba, salsa, and jazz with low to mid intensity. Slow, guided warm-up into fun, easy-to-follow sequences. Sweat without the grind. Eclectic playlist spanning bossa, samba, salsa, jazz, and global rhythms. Softly lit studio with shifting color lighting for uninhibited movement. All levels welcome. No experience needed.  
  
Bring water. Wear clothes you can move in. Bare feet, socks / grip socks. We use mats for the cool-down stretch. Mats are provided, or bring your own.  
  
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_  
  
  
About Movement Lab  
  
Movement Lab is an evolving set of classes and workshops that use rhythm, cross-modal dance, and somatic exploration to help you feel centered and clear. Born from a return to dance and a renewed sense of its importance. Current class offerings include Slow Flow Lab, Bossa Beats, and Inner Child Embodiment, with additional formats and aligned facilitators coming online as the project grows.  
  
Currently facilitated by Jen Miller, integrating somatic research with 15 years of live samba and dance performance, including appearances with Carlos Santana and EDC. Jen is training in trauma-informed yoga and movement practices including swing dance, salsa, kizomba, and tango, and holds certifications in sylvotherapy and Zumba instruction.  
  
For more info visit movementlab.co or IG: @movementlab.co

### Beat Procrastination and Creative Blocks

You know that state of mind where you forget about time and space and become completely focused on whatever you’re working on? That's called the FLOW, and it feels great but can be difficult to achieve consistently on your own. Through the power of mono-tasking, rituals, body doubling, and power breaks, we’ll reach the FLOW together, and you’ll be surprised by what you’ll have gotten done at the end of the day. So how does it work? We’ll have a series of 50-minute bursts of focused, mono-tasking work. Every burst will be separated by quick 10-minute breaks. After 2 bursts, we’ll have a longer break, where we’ll share a meal (remember to bring your own lunch) and socialize before diving into the last two bursts. Switching off notifications, focusing on a single task at a time, being surrounded by people doing the same, and not keeping track of time will help you reach the FLOW. Essentially, getting days' worth of work done in just four hours. Let's get those procrastinated tasks done and beat creative blocks TOGETHER! Here is what our session will look like: 10:30 - 10:45: introduction 10:45 - 11:35: first rush 11:35 - 11:45: short break 11:45 - 12:35: second rush 12:35 - 01:05: lunch break 01:05 - 01:55: third rush 01:55 - 02:05: short break 02:05 - 02:55: last rush 02:55 - 03:10: outro You are welcome to join for just one rush or stay for the whole session; we just ask you to enter or leave the room only during breaks to prevent distraction for the other participants.

### ✨ A Night of Hygge ✨

An Evening of Warmth, Connection, and Cozy Magic  
  
As winter settles in, we invite you to gather for A Night of Hygge — a celebration of comfort, friendship, and the quiet beauty of slowing down.  
  
What is Hygge?  
A Danish word with no direct translation, Hygge (pronounced “hoo-gah”) captures the feeling of coziness and contentment that comes from life’s simple pleasures — candlelight, warm drinks, cozy food, heartfelt conversation, and the gentle joy of being together.  
  
The Intention  
On this evening, we’ll create a space to rest and reconnect. Think soft blankets, glowing lights, soothing music, and the company of kind souls. Together we’ll share winter favorites, reflect on our hopes and dreams, and embrace the warmth that only true connection can bring.  
  
Bring your coziest blanket, a favorite treat or drink to share, and your open heart.  
Let’s gather to celebrate stillness, sweetness, and the simple magic of being together.

### Flow to Yoga Nidra

(In Person) In Flow to Yoga Nidra you'll begin the first half of class warming up with a gentle Vinyasa flow, and enjoy a long cool down of restorative poses. In the Vinyasa half you'll move in and out of yoga poses, carefully connecting breath to movement in an active practice to build heat, strength and mental focus. In the second half of class, you'll gently soften the body using props and and remain in each pose allowing the body to release while guided through a deeply relaxing Yoga Nidra. Yoga Nidra, also called yogic sleep, takes us effortlessly into a state of harmonious, restful being. From here, we can be healed, restored and awakened to our deepest, all-knowing, all-welcoming self.

### Yin Yoga 🎵

Yin yoga is a slow-paced, meditative style of yoga that focuses on holding poses for extended periods (3-10 minutes) to work deeply into the connective tissues, such as ligaments, tendons, and fascia. It emphasizes stillness, relaxation, and mindful attention to the body and breath. Unlike more active styles of yoga, yin yoga aims to promote flexibility, reduce stress, and improve overall well-being.
