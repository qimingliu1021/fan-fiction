# JOHNSON COUNTY

### [Flannel Fest](https://www.facebook.com/events/1249905130215009/?acontext=%7B%22event_action_history%22%3A[%7B%22mechanism%22%3A%22discovery_custom_tab%22%2C%22surface%22%3A%22bookmark%22%7D]%2C%22ref_notif_type%22%3Anull%7D)

*   Coralville Creekside Ballpark, Coralville
*   Saturday, November 1st from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.
*   _"...get outside for fall inspired activities including: flannel crafts, a variety of activities including an inflatable axe throwing game, bring your pup for a flannel photo booth and dog-friendly games, and consider registering for our Recreation Bags Tournament.
